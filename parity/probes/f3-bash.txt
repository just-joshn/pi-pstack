A backgrounded polling loop or watcher in bash never wakes this session, and it dies unseen. Run it as a pstack-shell watcher instead: subagent({ agent: "pstack-shell", task: "#pstack-shell-begin\n<script that exits on the event>\n#pstack-shell-end" }). Its exit wakes you at zero model cost (pi-runtime.md, Loops, goals, and wakes). Background servers and one-off jobs are not blocked, only polling loops and watchers.
